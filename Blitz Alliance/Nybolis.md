# Nybolis
- On the Nybb Peninsula.
- The name of the city is derived from an inscription that was dedicated to the nymphs, who were worshipped in times of antiquity. The muses were ancient fairies and are considered the source of knowledge embodied in the poetry, lyric songs and myths.

---
### Alexandrite Academy
- Magical academy and primary organized following of **Pelor**.
- Hyerarchy: The White and 7 Divisions:
    - The White | Chosen of Pelor.
    - Defenders | The military division.
    - Divers | Search bodies of water for lost treasure.
    - Faithful | In charge of the temple and healing the wounded.
    - Keepers | In charge of research, the archives and libraries.
    - Seekers | Seek intel and knowledge.
    - Watchers | The guards of the academy.
    - Whisperers | Spies.
